The Wizard's Woolly Dilemma (D&D 1st edition Adventure)

Some quests require steel and spell. This one requires a good shear.

The Wizard's Woolly Dilemma "Beware the wizard in sheep's clothing..." What starts as a peaceful rest on a dusty country road quickly turns into a battle for a wizard's legacy! Your party is interrupted by a frantic, unnervingly intelligent sheep carrying a wax-sealed scroll in its mouth. This is no ordinary farm animal—it’s Finethir Shinebright, a powerful Magic-User who was betrayed and polymorphed by his treacherous apprentice, Noke. Now, Noke has claimed Finethir's magical treehouse tower and is using a broken Wand of Polymorph to wreak havoc on the local wildlife. Finethir needs a brave (or easily bribed) band of adventurers to escort him home, defeat his usurper, and turn him back into a human.

All of my adventures are FREE! If you've enjoyed them, please consider supporting me. Thank you!
